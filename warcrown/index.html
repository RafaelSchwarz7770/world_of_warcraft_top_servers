About this server

WarCrown is a progressive Wrath of the Lich King Classic 3.4.5 private server built around the original Northrend progression while bringing modern systems and quality-of-life features to the classic experience.

The main realm, Crystalsong, is an x2 PvE realm for players who want steady progression without excessive rates or instant shortcuts.

The server runs on the Wrath Classic 3.4.5 64-bit client and includes modern client functionality alongside carefully developed PvE and PvP content.

PvE content

WarCrown follows progressive Wrath of the Lich King content with retail-style Northrend pacing.

  • Wrath Classic dungeons and raids
  • Dungeon Finder
  • Raid Finder for selected older raid content
  • Titan Rune Dungeons
  • Titan Rune Alpha
  • Titan Rune Beta
  • Titan Rune Gamma
  • Scenarios
  • Progressive endgame development
  • Scripted quests and open-world events

There are no artificial Northrend progression skips or free flying unlocks. The goal is to preserve the feeling of progressing through Wrath while improving the experience with modern systems.

Questions

What version does WarCrown run?

WarCrown runs Wrath of the Lich King Classic 3.4.5 on the 64-bit client.

What are the rates on Crystalsong?

Experience, quest experience, gold, quest gold, reputation, honor, battleground honor and normal drops are x2. Professions, arena points, legendary drops and repair cost stay at x1.

How do I start playing?

Open warcrown.org and connect with the WarCrown launcher.

Which PvP content is available?

Warsong Gulch, Arathi Basin, Alterac Valley, Eye of the Storm, Strand of the Ancients, Isle of Conquest, Wintergrasp, arenas and the Ring of Valor. The realm is on Arena Season 5.

Where is the promotional code used?

New players can use TCJU-4RWG-S5AP. Activate it in your faction capital.
