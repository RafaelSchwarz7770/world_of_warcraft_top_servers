About this list

AzerothTop is a monthly ranking of World of Warcraft private servers. Place on the board comes from votes cast during the current month, not from a paid slot. Realms from every major expansion can be listed: Vanilla 1.12, The Burning Crusade 2.4.3, Wrath of the Lich King 3.3.5 and 3.4.5, Cataclysm, Mists of Pandaria and Legion. Open a row to see rates, the realm and how to connect.

Questions

Why do people play on private servers?

Older expansions are no longer on retail, the realms are free to enter, and many of them add faster leveling or their own content. Players also stay for a smaller community and for the pace of Vanilla or Wrath.

How do I join a server from this list?

Open the realm's website, register an account, and download the client for that patch, such as 3.3.5 for Wrath or 2.4.3 for The Burning Crusade. Set realmlist.wtf to the address published by that server, then start the game and log in.

Is playing on a private server legal?

These realms are not licensed by Blizzard and break the game's terms of use, because they run the client world without permission. Lawsuits almost always target the people who operate a server, not the players, but the realms are still unofficial.

Which server is the best right now?

The realm with the most votes this month sits at number one. The order is not chosen by an editor and a rank cannot be bought.

Which expansion is most common?

Wrath of the Lich King, patch 3.3.5, still has the most realms and players. Vanilla and The Burning Crusade have steady communities of their own.

How does voting work?

A visitor can vote for one server every 12 hours. Votes are added as they come in and decide the order of the list.

When does the list reset?

The board resets on the first day of each month. Every vote count goes back to zero and the ranking starts over.

What is a World of Warcraft private server?

It is a realm run by players, separate from the official Blizzard service. You install a client for that expansion, register on the realm site and log in there. Most realms on this list are free to enter, with their own rates and rules.

How do I choose a server on this list?

Start with this month's vote rank, then match the expansion and the rates on the server page. Votes show that players came back to the list. They are not a paid placement. Read the patch, the rules and the download page before you install a client.
